# Portrait fix: Duncan Idaho

Replace one delivered file. Everything in the prologue brief (`docs/art/tutorial_assets_brief.md`, section 4 "Speaker portraits") still applies: same format, size, framing and style as the other portraits.

## The problem

`assets/ui/portraits/duncan.png` shows the same young man as `paul.png`: the same face, age, hair and build, only in a different uniform. In the game Duncan must never be mistaken for Paul.

## What to deliver

- **File:** `assets/ui/portraits/duncan.png`, replacing the current one.
- **Format:** RGBA PNG, **512 × 512**, the same head-and-shoulders framing, painted style and lighting as `gurney.png` and `thufir.png`, and read clearly at 120 px.

## Who he is (from the books)

- **Duncan Idaho**, swordmaster of House Atreides. He is a grown man in his prime, clearly **a good ten or more years older than Paul**, and he looks it.
- **Face:** broad and rounded, with high cheekbones and dark eyes. It's a soldier's face: weathered and confident, and warm rather than delicate.
- **Hair:** a thick cap of tight, black, curly hair, the book's "black goat hair". It's shorter and denser than Paul's loose waves.
- **Build:** heavy-shouldered and athletic, a fighter's neck.
- **Dress:** the dark-green Atreides uniform with the red hawk on the chest, a sword hilt over the shoulder. He should look like a man who has been in many fights.

## Checks before delivering

1. **Side by side with `paul.png`,** nobody could confuse them. Age, face shape, hair and build all differ at a glance.
2. **No likeness of any actor,** from any film or series. That applies to this portrait and is a standing rule for all art in the project. Design the face from the description above, not from a performer.
3. **The same painted style** as the other portraits.
